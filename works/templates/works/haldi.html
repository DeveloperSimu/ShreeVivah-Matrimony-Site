Haldi is a cherished pre-wedding ritual where loved ones gather to offer blessings and surround the couple with warmth. Turmeric, flowers, familiar songs, and playful laughter turn a simple ceremony into a memory the whole family shares.

Every family celebrates in its own way. What remains constant is the feeling: a joyful pause before the wedding, filled with care, colour, and anticipation.
