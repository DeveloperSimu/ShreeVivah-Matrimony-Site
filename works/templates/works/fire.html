More than a ritual.
A shared beginning.

In many Hindu wedding traditions, the sacred fire is at the centre of the ceremony. The couple makes offerings and takes their vows in its presence, surrounded by the blessings of family and community.

Practices and meanings vary between families and regions. At its heart, this moment reflects a commitment to walk forward together—with care, respect, and the support of those they love.
